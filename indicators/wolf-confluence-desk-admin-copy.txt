## Overview
Multi-factor confluence desk for NIFTY / BANKNIFTY / F&O charts on TradingView.
Scores Trend (EMA 21/55/200) + Momentum (RSI) + Volatility (ATR) + Participation (Volume) + Structure/Pullback.
A signal fires only when the confluence score reaches your threshold (default 4/5).

## Important
This is a confluence SCORE tool — not a guaranteed accuracy system.
No indicator can promise 80–90% win rate. Use as context with your own risk rules.

## How to use
1. Add on 5m / 15m (intraday) or 1h (swing).
2. Watch EMAs + the top-right score table (Bull vs Bear /5).
3. No buy/sell triangle signals — context / score only.
4. Optional: enable NSE session filter (09:15–15:30 IST) in settings.

## Best with
- Higher-timeframe lean first (15m under 1h trend).
- Avoid chasing when ATR filter shows Quiet.
- Journal your own decisions — improve YOUR edge, don't trust a fixed %.
